A transaction is one document or email that a process runs on. Upload a file, then watch its status.

**1.** Open **Home**. The **Transactions** screen lists every transaction with its **Status** (Completed, Failed, Inprogress).

![Screenshot](/media/user-guide/image34.webp)

*The Transactions screen — each row shows a Status.*

**2.** To add a single file, click **Upload File**. Choose the **File Type**, optionally Select Process, browse to the file, then **Upload**.

![Screenshot](/media/user-guide/image35.webp)

*Upload File.*

**3.** To upload a packaged batch, click **Upload Transaction**, browse to the **.zip** file, then **Upload**.

![Screenshot](/media/user-guide/image36.webp)

*Upload Transaction (.zip).*

**4.** To classify a single PDF, click **Classify Document**, enter the **Job Number**, select the process, upload the PDF, then **Classify**.

![Screenshot](/media/user-guide/image37.webp)

*Classify Document.*

**5.** Watch the **Status** column. Use the Actions icons on the right to view, open the **timeline**, or download the result.

:::note

**View batch details:** To see the details inside a transaction, open it and click Expand on the batch — not the direct link. Expanding opens the sub-batch, where you see all the tools and only the keys the document needs (blank, unneeded keys are hidden). From here you can also see the prompt directly, and the project by clicking the info button.

:::
